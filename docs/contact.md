# Contact

For general inquiries or professional support, email [info@chojs.io](mailto:info@chojs.io).

- **Framework behavior or feature requests.** Open an issue with the package name, runtime and a small example of the behavior you want to discuss. [Framework issues](https://github.com/cho-js/cho/issues)
- **Documentation feedback.** Share the page URL and the step or example that needs clarification. [Documentation issues](https://github.com/cho-js/docs/issues)
- **Security reports.** Send vulnerability details privately to the maintainers. [Private reporting form](https://github.com/cho-js/cho/security/advisories/new)
- **Project updates.** Follow CHO on [X](https://x.com/cho_framework) or browse [GitHub releases](https://github.com/cho-js/cho/releases).
